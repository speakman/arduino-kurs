#### Important (should fix)

**1. Stale dark-mode rgba in active CSS — `.warn-box` background**
- **Where:** `presentation/styles/main.css:174`
- **What:** `background: rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.08);` is the **old** `--ak-danger` value (`#ff3366`). The new danger token is `#be123c`.
- **Why this matters:** `.warn-box` is used on slides 21, 31, 33. Visually inconsistent with the new palette.
- **Fix:** `background: color-mix(in srgb, var(--ak-danger) 8%, transparent);`

**2. Cover slide on-screen contrast risk (not visible in PDF review)**
- **Where:** `presentation/styles/main.css:213-231` (cover layout) and `presentation/slides.md:33-45`
- **What:** Cover h1 uses `color: var(--ak-cover-fg)` = `#0a0e14` (near-black) over a hero photo of a dark Arduino. Overlay gradient at title (top, 25% bg) leaves ~75% photo show-through. Bottom-corner labels use `opacity-60` on near-black with overlay at 88% bg, giving ~2.3:1.
- **Why:** In live presentation mode (npm run dev) the contrast may fail. The PDF render hides this because html.print suppresses the photo.
- **Fix options:** Increase overlay strength at title region, add text-shadow halo, or switch cover-fg to white when photo is shown.

**3. Stale dark-mode rgba on (likely unused) selectors**
- `presentation/styles/main.css:158` — `.sp.unlocked` gradient uses `rgba(0, 255, 209, 0.04)` (old cyan)
- `presentation/styles/main.css:265` — `.dots-bg` background uses `rgba(0, 255, 209, 0.08)` (old cyan)
- **Why:** Neither selector is referenced in active slides.md/components, so stale colors don't render. Cleanup candidate.

**4. Cover hero photo may be near-invisible in light mode**
- **Where:** `presentation/styles/main.css:213-224`
- **What:** Light gradient over dark photo blends to faded/grey-washed look — inverse of the old design.
- **Fix:** Beyond #2, consider regenerating cover-arduino-hero.jpg for light theme, or inverting gradient direction.
