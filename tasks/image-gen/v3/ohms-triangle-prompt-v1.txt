Create a pedagogical Ohms-law mnemonic triangle for an Arduino electronics course in soft 3D editorial style.

REQUIRED STRUCTURE — equilateral triangle with three letter-labels positioned for the "cover-the-variable" pedagogical trick:

- TOP vertex (apex): the letter "U" in large bold monospace, in Arduino-teal accent color (#00747d). U represents Voltage (Spänning).
- BOTTOM-LEFT corner: the letter "R" in large bold monospace, dark color (#0a0e14). R represents Resistance (Resistans).
- BOTTOM-RIGHT corner: the letter "I" in large bold monospace, dark color (#0a0e14). I represents Current (Ström).
- A horizontal divider line crossing the middle of the triangle, separating U (top) from R · I (bottom). The divider has a small subtle "·" multiplication dot in its center.
- Subtle subtitle BELOW the triangle (outside): "U = R · I" in small monospace teal, light opacity.

PEDAGOGICAL POINT: cover any one letter with your finger, and the formula tells you the answer:
- Cover U → see "R · I" → multiply
- Cover R → see U over I → divide U by I
- Cover I → see U over R → divide U by R
