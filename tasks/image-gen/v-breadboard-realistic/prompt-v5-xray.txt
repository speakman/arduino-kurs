INTERNAL LAYER (visible through the translucent plastic):
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Beneath the plastic, the viewer sees the phosphor-bronze metal contact
strips. These are the actual electrical conductors that make a

METAL STRIP MATERIAL: warm phosphor-bronze color (#b8865d to #8b6940
gradient), with subtle metallic sheen. Slightly darker where the plastic
above is denser, slightly brighter where catching light. NOT shiny chrome,
NOT pure copper — phosphor-bronze has a warm muted golden-brown tone.

LAYOUT OF METAL STRIPS — this is electrically critical and must be
correct:

1. TERMINAL STRIPS (in the central part of the board):
   - For EACH of the 30 numbered rows, there are TWO separate short
     horizontal metal strips:
       (a) one running under columns a-b-c-d-e (covers 5 holes)
       (b) one running under columns f-g-h-i-j (covers 5 holes)
   - Each strip is approximately 12mm long × 3-4mm wide as seen through
     the plastic. Strip is positioned exactly under the 5 holes of that
     row's column-bank.
   - The strip for row N (a-e) is COMPLETELY SEPARATE from the strip
     for row N (f-j) — there is a clear physical gap between them
     aligned with the center groove.
   - The strip for row N is COMPLETELY SEPARATE from the strip for row
     N+1 — there is a small visible gap (~1mm) of plastic between
     adjacent rows where no metal exists. EVERY row has its own
     independent pair of strips.
   - Total terminal strips visible: 60 (30 rows × 2 strips per row).

2. POWER RAIL STRIPS (along the four rail edges):
   - Along each "+" rail (left edge AND right edge of the board): ONE
     long single continuous metal strip running the FULL length of the
     board, from near row 1 to near row 30. Approximately 70mm long ×

   - Along each "−" rail: same — one long continuous metal strip.
   - Total power rail strips visible: 4 (left +, left −, right +, right −).
   - The left "+" rail strip is NOT connected to the right "+" rail
     strip (no metal bridge between them).
   - The "+" and "−" strips on the same side are also separate from
     each other.

3. CONTACT FINGERS (optional fine detail, if the rendering allows):
