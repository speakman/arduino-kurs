INTERNAL METAL CONTACT STRIPS — CRITICAL GEOMETRY:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The metal contacts inside this breadboard are THIN SHEET-METAL U-CHANNELS
(also called channel-shaped contact strips). Each contact strip is
formed by taking a thin flat strip of silvery sheet metal (nickel-plated
steel or tin-plated phosphor-bronze) and bending it into a long U-shape
in cross-section, like a long narrow trough or gutter.

CROSS-SECTION (looking at one strip end-on, like viewing a section cut):

       │         │          ← two thin vertical walls (~0.4mm thick)
       │         │
       │         │            ← the channel interior is OPEN at the top
       └─────────┘          ← thin flat base (closed bottom)

       ←  3mm  →

The U-channel has:
- A flat thin base/bottom (~3mm wide, ~0.4mm thick sheet metal)
- Two thin vertical side-walls rising from the base (~2mm tall, ~0.4mm
  thick)
- An OPEN TOP (no roof, no closure) — this is where component legs
  slot down into the channel from above
- Total cross-section: ~3mm wide × 2mm tall, with very thin walls

NOT a solid cylindrical rod (that was my earlier error — round metal
sticks are wrong)
NOT a thick rectangular block (also wrong)
NOT a flat metal plate (also wrong)

YES, a thin metallic CHANNEL/TROUGH shape, hollow inside, opening
upward, like a long narrow rain gutter.

VIEWED FROM ABOVE through the translucent plastic, each U-channel
strip appears as a thin rectangular outline with a visible open slot
running its length — you see the two vertical wall edges and the base
of the channel through the opening.

MATERIAL: Silvery polished sheet metal — chrome / nickel-plated steel
/ tin-plated metal. Cool silver tone (#c8c8c8 to #909090 with metallic
specular highlights). NOT warm bronze, NOT brass, NOT copper. Subtle
patina or very mild oxidation tint allowed (gentle golden-brown stains
in places, suggesting mass-produced electronic component, not pristine
chrome).

LAYOUT — must be electrically and visually correct:

1. POWER RAIL U-CHANNELS — TWO parallel U-channels on EACH SIDE:
   - LEFT SIDE: TWO long U-channels running vertically the full length
     of the board (~70mm), arranged side-by-side and parallel. Outer
     channel = "+" rail (under red printed line); inner channel = "−"
     rail (under blue printed line). They are PHYSICALLY SEPARATE —
     plastic between them. Each channel opens upward.
   - RIGHT SIDE: same — TWO long parallel U-channels, outer = "+",
     inner = "−". Same upward openings.
   - TOTAL: FOUR long U-channels on the periphery.
   - LEFT "+" channel NOT connected to RIGHT "+" channel.
   - "+" and "−" channels on the same side NOT connected to each other.

2. TERMINAL STRIP U-CHANNELS — 30 PAIRS of short horizontal U-channels
   in the central area:
   - For EACH of the 30 numbered rows, TWO short U-channels:
       (a) one running horizontally under columns a-b-c-d-e (~12mm long)
       (b) one running horizontally under columns f-g-h-i-j (~12mm long)
   - Each short U-channel has same cross-section as power rails (3mm
     wide × 2mm tall, opening upward, walls ~0.4mm thick).
   - Row N (a-e) channel COMPLETELY SEPARATE from row N (f-j) channel
     — clear plastic gap aligned with center groove.
   - Row N channel COMPLETELY SEPARATE from row N+1 channel — visible
     ~1mm gap of plastic between adjacent rows.
   - TOTAL: 60 short U-channels (30 rows × 2 channels per row).

3. ALL U-channels visible THROUGH the translucent plastic — slightly
   muted but clearly identifiable as the metallic hero element.
   Realistic 3D form, metallic specular highlights from studio lighting,
   subtle shadows in the channel interior (the inside of the U is
   slightly darker because less light reaches the bottom of the channel).
